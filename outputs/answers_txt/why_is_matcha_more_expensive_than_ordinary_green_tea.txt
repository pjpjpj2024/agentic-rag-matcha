USER QUERY
Why is matcha more expensive than ordinary green tea?

FINAL ANSWER
Matcha is more expensive than ordinary green tea due to its labor-intensive production process and specialized cultivation methods. After harvesting, the leaves are steamed to prevent oxidation, then manually processed to remove stems and veins. The remaining leaves are ground slowly using traditional stone mills, a time-consuming method that contributes to its higher cost compared to typical loose-leaf green tea. Additionally, matcha plants are cultivated under shade, which enhances chlorophyll levels and produces the vibrant green color. Only the youngest buds and top leaves are handpicked, further increasing labor costs and setting it apart from ordinary tea production.
